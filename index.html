How OVOSKG came forth

OVOSKG grew from a practical belief: clothing should not force people to fit into standard options. It should be shaped around the person wearing it. The company developed around custom tailoring, thoughtful finishing and direct relationships with clients who wanted clothing made specifically for them.

Today the focus spans bespoke suits for men and women, luxury men's kaftans and custom pieces, supported by a physical mini boutique and a growing digital customer experience.
